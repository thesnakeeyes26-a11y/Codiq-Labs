FAQ

Common Questions

Get answers to how we build our AI automations and custom websites.

Which automation tools do you build with?

We primarily construct workflows inside n8n, Make, and Retool, depending on your database complexity. For script-level tasks, we write native Python or Node.js scripts executed on isolated cloud workers to ensure cost-efficiency and performance.

How do the AI voice agents sound?

We build our voice agent backends on Vapi and Retell AI, driving speech synthesis via ElevenLabs and Cartesia. The resulting systems achieve sub-second latency and feature organic speech elements, including natural pauses, hums, and contextually accurate conversational rhythms.

Do we own the custom automation code?

Absolutely. Once the project is delivered and handed over, you own the complete intellectual property (IP) of the workflows, code scripts, custom API configurations, and designs we develop for you.

What is your standard project delivery speed?

Landing page and custom automation developments typically conclude in 10-14 days. More intricate systems—such as multi-tier database synchronizations or conversational voice integrations—generally span 3-5 weeks depending on custom testing requirements.

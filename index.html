About Codiq Labs

Technology that works for you,
and keeps your data with you.

Codiq Labs is a technology company with two sides. For businesses, we build automation, websites, apps and voice agents. For the world, we build our own products, designed around one simple promise: your data stays yours.

WHO WE ARE

Codiq Labs is a technology company built on a simple belief: powerful technology should never cost people their privacy. We work on two fronts at once. We serve businesses with practical automation and software, and we create our own global products that prove intelligence and privacy can live together.

Every system we make, whether it is an automation for a growing business or a product for people everywhere, starts with the same question: what does this really need to know, and does any of it need to leave your hands?

WHAT WE DO

For businesses, we automate repetitive work, build premium websites and apps, and deploy AI voice agents that handle conversations around the clock. The goal is always the same: less manual effort, faster work, and a team that can focus on growth.

For the world, we are building products like Aivus AI (coming soon), an assistant designed to run fully offline on your own device, with no cloud server and nothing sent over the internet. Two sides, one standard: build it well, and keep it private.

See services & process →
OUR GOAL

Our main goal is privacy. Most smart tools today ask you to send your conversations, files and decisions to someone else’s server. We believe that trade is unnecessary, and we are building the alternative.

At the same time, many businesses are still struggling with outdated tools and manual work. We want to give every one of them a fresh touch of new technology, built carefully, explained clearly and never at the cost of their data.
